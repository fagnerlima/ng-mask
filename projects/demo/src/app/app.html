ng-mask

Input masks for Angular, with Brazilian masks ready to use.

  • 0 runtime dependencies: no jQuery, no mask library, not even tslib.
  • Brazilian presets: CPF, CNPJ, phone, CEP, dates, money and license plates.
  • Alphanumeric CNPJ mask and CPF/CNPJ check-digit validators. 
npm install @fagnerlima/ng-mask

Brazilian presets

The CPF is the Brazilian taxpayer number of a person and the CNPJ the registry number of a company. From July 2026, new CNPJs may contain letters in their first 12 characters; the brCnpj mask accepts them and writes them in upper case.
